Privacy Policy

Spend Zen: Expense Tracker · Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Spend Zen app (“the app”) uses. We keep it simple: the app has no accounts, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, bank login or location.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter — transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, bills, recurring payments, categories, notes and your settings (currency, theme, notification choices) — is stored only on your device. We do not receive, upload or have access to it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Android app backup is turned off, so the data is not copied to Google Drive or any cloud by the app.

2. App lock

If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored on your device only. Biometric unlock (fingerprint or face) is handled by Android; the app never sees your biometric data.

3. Receipts and exports

If you attach a receipt image to a transaction, the app keeps only a reference to the image you picked, on your device. If you export your data (CSV, PDF or Excel), the file is created only when you ask for it and is saved to the location you choose. We do not send exported files anywhere.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The amounts, categories and notes you enter in the app are never shared with advertisers.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android phones).

5. Firebase

The app uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database) to download its ad settings and update information. This request contains no personal information from you, but, like any internet request, it exposes your IP address to Google. See the Firebase privacy information.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and settings.
  • Notifications – optional; lets the app show bill and budget reminders on your device.
  • Biometric – optional; used only for App lock.

The app does not use your contacts, microphone, camera or location.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob and Firebase), as described above.

8. Children

The app is a general-audience money-tracking tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

All network requests made by the app use HTTPS. The data you enter lives on your device only, so keeping your device secure (screen lock and the app’s App lock) keeps it safe.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
